How a week's chart becomes a score

TrueAchievements lists the 40 most-played Xbox games each week.It ranks by play time across a sample of about 3.1 million Xbox accounts.
→
A game placed r earns 41 − r points that week.Outside the top 40 it earns nothing.1st = 40 · 10th = 31 · 40th = 1
→
Adding up the weeks gives each game's total.A game that charts high for a long time wins, like points in a track meet.
→
Dividing by weeks since release makes it fair.Baldur's Gate 3, Outlaws, Oblivion Remastered and Outer Worlds 2 came out during the tracking period.

About this data

  • Ranks come from the weekly TrueAchievements Xbox chart, and store places from the Microsoft Store most popular Xbox games list. Both are typed in by hand each week.
  • This is an unofficial page, not made or endorsed by TrueAchievements, Microsoft or any game's publisher.
  • No row for the week of 2024-12-29 (between 2024-12-22 and 2025-01-05).
  • 2026-09-27 to 2026-10-07 is 10 days, not 7 (2026-10-07 is a Wednesday).
  • 2026-10-07 to 2026-10-12 is 5 days, not 7 (2026-10-12 is a Monday).
  • No TrueAchievements data for 2026-09-20, 2026-09-27; those weeks count for no game.
  • The spreadsheet this tracker started from scored rank − 41, so 1st place gave −40 and every total was negative. This page uses 41 − rank, so 1st place gives 40 points.
  • That spreadsheet's Cyberpunk 2077 total (818) left out two weeks: 2025-08-31 (rank 40) and 2026-11-09 (rank 13). With them the total is 847.
  • Weeks 2026-09-13 to 2026-09-27 had "NO TA DATA" typed in the Microsoft Store column. 2026-09-13 still has TrueAchievements ranks and an article link, so the note is read as "no store figure" for all three weeks, and 2026-09-20 and 2026-09-27 also have no TrueAchievements data.
  • Points per week counts weeks from the game's first playable date on Xbox (early access where there was one), and only weeks with TrueAchievements data.
